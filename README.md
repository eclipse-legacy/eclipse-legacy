## About

We are a team of student engineers from Begum Rokeya University,Rangpur, Bangladesh, empowering the next generation of innovators through coding and robotics. Our focus: **robotics, control systems, power electronics, and intelligent automation**.

We share STEM education, DIY projects, and AI insights.

## Achievement

🥈 **2nd place**, National-Level Project Showcasing Competition at Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST), Saidpur.

## Featured Projects

### 1. Transformer Monitoring & Control System
- ESP32-based monitoring of voltage, current, temperature, and humidity
- Detects faults early and cuts the load automatically through a relay
- No manual checking of the transformer required

### 2. AI-Based Automation System
- Control devices by voice, gesture, typing, or a single click
- Runs on Firebase with ESP32/ESP8266
- Asks for confirmation before performing risky actions
